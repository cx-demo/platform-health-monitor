Revision D (draft) publishes one derived readiness state per platform, shown in Figure 2, under rule SYS‑4412‑R1. It does not publish the evidence behind that state. The two questions below remain unanswerable from the interface.

  • Is this fault mission-critical, or merely noted? A subsystem reports only that it is operational or that it is not. Consequence is absent, so severity is guessed downstream.

    Reserved

  • How old is this reading? A temperature is published without an observation time. A value recorded seconds ago and one recorded an hour ago are indistinguishable on this sheet.

    Reserved

These voids are drawn deliberately. They are a finding, not a defect in this sheet. Closing them is a further interface change: it needs a new ICD‑PHM‑002 revision and a consumer impact assessment before merge. Until then, the honest rendering of an unanswerable question is an empty field with its dimensions shown.

Read-only. This sheet issues no command and holds no tasking authority. It performs one request, GET /v2/platforms, and draws the result.

Source of figures. Published record for any platform is available directly at /v2/platforms/{platformId}. The full generated interface is at /v2/docs, /v2/redoc and /v2/openapi.json. The revision C surface remains at /platforms without readiness fields.

Fleet data is synthetic and fixed in src/repository.py for demonstration. Platform designations, identifiers and readings are authored, not observed, and represent no real platform.
