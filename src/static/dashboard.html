Figure 3 · What this interface cannot answer

Reserved · not published at revision C

The three questions below decide whether a platform may be tasked. Revision C carries no field that answers any of them, so each consumer answers them privately, from its own assumptions, and two consumers reading the same payload may reasonably disagree.

  • Is this fault mission-critical, or merely noted? A subsystem reports only that it is operational or that it is not. Consequence is absent, so severity is guessed downstream.

    Reserved

  • How old is this reading? A temperature is published without an observation time. A value recorded seconds ago and one recorded an hour ago are indistinguishable on this sheet.

    Reserved

  • May this platform be tasked? No published field states a readiness conclusion. The judgement is left to every consumer independently, which is the condition SYS-4412 exists to end.

    Reserved

These voids are drawn deliberately. They are a finding, not a defect in this sheet. Closing them is an interface change: it advances ICD‑PHM‑002 to revision D and requires a consumer impact assessment before merge. Until that change is approved, the honest rendering of an unanswerable question is an empty field with its dimensions shown.
